4. RENDER RECEIPT BOUNDARY
============================================================

UnrealRenderReceipt is engine-neutral and evidence-bound. It is issued from
verified render-job inspection evidence rather than from a submission response.

UnrealRenderReceiptStore provides durable persistence for the receipt identity.
The Unreal runtime render-job registry remains in-memory.

Cross-process Unreal render-job recovery has NOT been implemented.
Do not describe receipt persistence as runtime job persistence.

The persisted receipt commits to the verified evidence through the existing
canonical evidence-digest mechanism.
