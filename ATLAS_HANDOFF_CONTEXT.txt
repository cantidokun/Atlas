============================================================
1. PROJECT DIRECTION
============================================================

Atlas is an AI-assisted virtual-production and digital-twin platform focused exclusively on soccer-field-related digital twins and their production pipeline.

Dedicated photogrammetry software creates the initial reconstruction. Blender analyzes, cleans, corrects, optimizes, and prepares that reconstruction. Unreal is a downstream controlled production environment.

Atlas owns the canonical Digital Twin. Blender files, Unreal projects/levels, render jobs, receipts, and output artifacts are production representations/state, not canonical identity.

    -> establish what actually happened
