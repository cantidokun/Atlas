# Adapter Boundary

Atlas owns the canonical Digital Twin. Blender, Unreal, and photogrammetry are external production or intake systems connected through explicit boundaries.

Representations bind to an Atlas Twin and source revision. Tool-native IDs and coordinate conversion stay inside adapters. Tool state never becomes canonical implicitly.

Photogrammetry output enters Atlas as reconstruction intake and requires Atlas analysis, cleanup, identity evaluation, and validation before canonical promotion.

The future Unreal Agent operates through the same boundary rather than owning Twin state.
